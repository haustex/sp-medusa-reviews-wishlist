### 0.1.0

First release: verified product reviews (rating, title, text, photos, locale), moderation with store replies and
bulk actions, stored rating aggregates, review-request e-mails with single-use magic links (guest reviews),
one-click unsubscribe, Admin feed notifications, anti-abuse (rate limit, honeypot, sanitising), JSON-LD helper,
customer + guest wishlists with merge, share links and per-product counts, privacy handling on customer deletion.
Tested against Medusa 2.19.0 and 2.22.x.
